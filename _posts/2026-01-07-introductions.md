Alright, here we go.

My name, believe it or not, is Reid Bacon. I am a husband to `<borat`> my wife `</borat>`, an ultimate frisbee player (which I affectionately refer to as "frisbee"), and a software engineer.

I really love computers. My mom got my brother into video games in the early 90s, which I picked up when I was born shortly after (mostly as player 2), and my dad encouraged us to evolve into master typists... shout-out Mario and Mavis, the two best typing teachers I've ever had.

I have fond memories of working with my brother to set up our internet after moves or troubleshooting outages. I'd often be on the phone with the support staff of whatever ISP we had, relaying instructions to him, confusing our helper with my pre-pubescent high-pitched squeaking -- "Yes ma'am, please plug in your modem now."

That love evolved into a career, after some high school programming (shout-out Mrs Lewis!) and collegiate specialization (shout-out Dr Lewis (unrelated)!) I've now spent 10 years in "the industry", building software solutions for a large bank. I squatted on reidbacon.com awhile ago and decided it was time to play around here.

My intention for this site is unclear -- it's just a GitHub "Pages" site right now, which feels a little bare-bones, but I felt like I needed a place to get my thoughts out. I don't really intend for this to be a showcase of my software engineering, although I am proud that I was able to spin this up relatively easily, but I also don't feel like it's particularly impressive for "the industry." Do I add "buts" in every sentence?!? Be proud of yourself, dammit!

I have things that I'd like to say, and I wish I could be paid for them, but I also think there is immense value in open-source knowledge. I hope to be able to share my understanding of the world around me, some experiences I've had and how they've shaped me, and maybe even some wisdoms that I've learned both the hard way and the easy way. I have a lot of sayings, so maybe some posts are expansions on those.

Anyway, about time to wrap this one up. Thanks for joining. Hope to see you next time.

Yours, Reid
